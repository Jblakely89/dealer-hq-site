Credit & F&I

Pull credit, send the application, and sell F&I products without leaving the deal.

700

700CreditCredit reports

Run a soft-pull prequalification or a full credit report from the deal. Results land on the customer card, and managers see the full report.

R1

RouteOneLender network

Send the credit application to your lenders from the desk. Decisions come back onto the deal.

DT

DealertrackLender network

Send the same application through Dealertrack F&I. Each store picks its lender network, and a store can run both.

PEN

PENF&I product rating

Service contracts, GAP and other products come in through the Provider Exchange Network with live rates. They're presented on the F&I menu and contracted with the deal.

DS

DocuSignE-signature

Values & parts

Book values on every unit, and accessories that actually fit it.

JDP

J.D. Power ConnectUnit values & bookouts

PU

Parts UnlimitedAccessories catalog

Pull accessories from the live catalog, checked for fitment against the unit. Add them to a deal on the desk or let the customer add them online.

Phone

Every call lands on the customer card it belongs to.

ZP

Zoom PhoneCalls & voicemail

Calls are matched to the customer, recorded, transcribed and read for what was promised. Missed calls and voicemails alert the right person while the lead is still warm.

Marketing

Leads come in, conversations stay together, and the ad platforms learn who actually bought.

M

MetaFacebook & Instagram

Lead Ads land in the CRM as they're submitted. Messenger and Instagram conversations are answered from HQ, and ad spend sits next to the deals it produced.

Google AdsSearch & Performance Max

S

ShopifyOnline parts store

WP

WorldpayCard payments

Take deposits and payments through hosted card checkout at the cashier. Receipts post to the deal and the ledger.

ADPPayroll

HQ builds the payroll register from hours and commissions, and a manager approves it. It then goes to ADP to be paid.
