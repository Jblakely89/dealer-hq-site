Capabilities

Everything the store runs on. Built in.

Each department gets a My Day board like the one above: the day's goal, what is due and the next move, with every record one tap away. Here is what sits behind them.

Sell front of the house
AI inside

CRM + Riley AI

Every web form, Messenger thread, text, call and walk-in lands on one customer card, and Riley works it around the clock.

  • Lead routing, rotation and response-time tracking
  • Riley answers, follows up and books the visit
  • Calls and texts graded against your process
  • A rep's whole day on one My Day board

Desking, F&I & credit

Structure the deal, pull credit and present the menu without leaving the customer's record.

  • Worksheet with trade, fees, taxes and rebates
  • Credit pulls and lender submissions from the deal
  • F&I menu with live product rating
  • Stips board so nothing funds late

E-sign

The deal's forms are filled from the record and signed in the store or from the customer's phone.

  • State and lender forms filled automatically
  • In-person or remote signing
  • Signed copies filed to the deal jacket
  • Readiness check before anything goes out

Inventory & pricing

Every unit from intake to sold, priced against the market and pushed everywhere it sells.

  • Readiness: prepped, photographed, online
  • Market pricing and repricing as units age
  • Feeds to your website and ad catalogs
  • Listing copy checked against ad policy

Buying

A real CRM for the units you buy: sellers, trades and dealer bids worked like leads.

  • "Sell us your vehicle" leads straight to the board
  • Appraise, offer and track the counter
  • Lien payoff and title tracked to the end
  • Pickup booked on the dispatch board

Website & live chat

Your site served from your own inventory, with chat and pre-approval that land on the customer's card.

  • Units go live when ready, drop when sold
  • Quick quote and full credit app on every unit
  • Chat that answers in seconds and hands off
  • Every visit tied back to the lead
Run back of the house

Service & parts

Repair orders, the tech board and the parts counter on the same customer and unit.

  • Write, dispatch and close ROs
  • Tech time and open-RO aging on a board
  • Parts lookup, fitment and special orders
  • Recon that tells sales when a unit is ready

Dispatch & transport

Every pickup, transfer and delivery planned, routed and tracked, with the customer kept in the loop.

  • Runs built with route order and trailer space
  • Booking links customers pick their own time
  • Driver view on a phone: stops, photos, signatures
  • Status texts at every step

Accounting, GL & bank feed

Sold deals, receipts and bills post themselves to your general ledger, and the bank feed matches them.

  • Deals post to the GL when they close
  • A/R, A/P and funding receivables tracked
  • Live bank feed for reconciliation
  • Monthly statements from the same ledger

Reports

Every department's goal and pace each morning, built from the same rows the work happens in.

  • My Day boards for every role
  • Weekly 1-on-1 scorecards by department
  • Ad spend traced to leads, apps and sales
  • Company pulse and month plan for the owner
