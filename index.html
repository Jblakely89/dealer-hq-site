The modules

Everything a rooftop runs on, sharing one set of records.

Seven core modules run the store. Eight add-ons are upgrades you switch on when they earn it, priced per module on top of your base. Every one reads and writes the same customers, units and deals, so nothing gets re-keyed and nothing gets lost between systems.

Core modules

Your base. Pick any, priced per rooftop.

CRM

Every lead, every channel, one record. Your process, not ours.

Website forms, Messenger, Marketplace, texts, chat, phone calls, walk-ins and dealer buy bids land on the same customer card with the ad that produced them. Then it works the lead the way you manage.

  • Build your own sales process: stages, rules, who owns what
  • Tasks created automatically from texts and phone calls
  • Source and ad-level attribution on every lead
  • Pre-approvals stamped to the contact and the deal
  • A buying-side CRM for sellers, not just buyers
AI · Ask HQ

An assistant that lives inside your numbers.

Ask HQ works from your database, not a generic model of a dealership. It answers with your units, your grosses and your leads, then does the work.

  • Daily read: what sold, how old it was, what it made
  • Writes listings and follow-ups in your voice
  • Drafts the write-up when a trend shows up
  • Audits ads, bots and reps against real outcomes
  • Every answer traceable to the rows it came from
Desking

Desk the deal where the customer already is.

Payments, trades, rebates, fees and the F&I menu on one screen. Push to your lenders and your credit bureau without leaving the deal.

  • Payment grid with term, rate and money-down scenarios
  • Credit pulls and lender submissions from the deal
  • F&I menu with live provider rating
  • State forms, e-signature and a stips checklist
  • Front and back gross visible in real time
DMS

Units, service and parts in a database you own.

Inventory from intake to sold, repair orders and technician time, parts and the paperwork that closes a deal, all in one system.

  • Unit readiness: prepped, photographed, online, priced
  • Service board with open RO aging and tech productivity
  • Parts and accessories on the same ticket
  • Accounting handoff to the ledger you already use
  • Role-based permissions by department and store
Transport & Logistics

Dispatch every pickup, transfer and delivery.

A lane board for drivers, a schedule the sales floor can see, and a paper trail for every unit that moves.

  • Pickups from public sellers and dealer buy runs
  • Store-to-store transfers with the units attached
  • Customer deliveries tied to the signed deal
  • Driver view on the phone with photos and signatures
  • Miles, hours and cost per move on the report
Website

Your site, served from your inventory.

No third-party feed lag, no vendor markup on your own listings. Units appear the moment they're ready and drop the moment they sell.

  • Inventory, pricing and photos straight from the DMS
  • Pre-approval and full credit app on every unit page
  • Search pages you can publish yourself, daily
  • Feeds to Meta and Google vehicle catalogs
  • Visitor tracking that flows back to the CRM record
Detailed reporting

The numbers you manage by, every morning.

Sales, service, buying, titles and marketing scorecards built from your own tables. The same reports we run our two stores on, with the goal and the pace next to every figure.

  • Weekly 1-on-1 scorecards per department
  • Ad spend to approvals to deals, per ad
  • Wallboards for the floor and the shop
  • Export or query any report directly

Add-ons

Upgrades priced per module on top of your base. Switch each one on when it earns it.

Title management

Know where every title is. Put it on a report.

Most systems have no place to record whether you actually hold the title. HQ tracks every unit's title from acquisition to the customer, and flags the ones that are late.

  • Title in hand, in transit, lien pending, missing
  • Days outstanding per unit, per store, per buyer
  • Alerts before a title blocks a delivery
  • Missing-titles report for the weekly 1-on-1
Marketing conversions

Every ad dollar traced to an approval and a deal.

Conversion tracking across Google, Facebook, Bing, Marketplace and your website, stamped onto the lead so you know which ad actually sold a unit.

  • Google Ads, Meta, Bing and Marketplace in one report
  • Pre-approval and deal events sent back to each platform
  • Marketplace posting and instant reply on every unit
  • Kill losing ads weekly with real cost per deal
Optimized pricing · premium

The right number on every unit, backed by the market.

Every dealer in your area scraped daily, your own sold history, and JD Power values, combined into a list price, a floor, a buy offer and a trade number.

  • Competitor inventory, prices and days-on-site
  • Your sold data: what moved, at what gross, how fast
  • JD Power and market comps on the same screen
  • Repricing suggestions as units age
Alerts & accountability

Your data, fed to you. Trigger events, not spreadsheets.

Set the triggers that matter and let HQ watch them: a lead sitting too long, a follow-up missed, a title overdue. When a trend takes shape, the write-up is already drafted.

  • Lead-aging and no-reply alerts to the right manager
  • Trend detection per rep, per department
  • Write-ups drafted from the record, ready to sign
  • Escalation rules you define
Call & text auditing

Every call and text graded against your process.

HQ listens to the calls and reads the texts, then grades each one against your rubric: greeting, discovery, appointment ask, follow-up. Coaching, not guessing.

  • Your rubric, your scoring, per department
  • Every conversation scored with the moments flagged
  • Rep trends over time feed accountability
  • Coaching notes generated for the 1-on-1
Review funnels

Turn delivered units into five-star reviews.

A request at the right moment, routed to Google, with unhappy customers caught first and every review attributed to the rep who earned it.

  • Timed requests after delivery and service
  • Unhappy customers routed to a manager, not to Google
  • Per-rep and per-store review counts on the scorecard
  • Referral capture on the same page
Digital retail

Let the customer build the deal at midnight.

Payment, trade value, protection products and a real pre-approval on the unit page. The deal lands on the desk already structured.

  • Live payments with your rates and fees
  • Trade appraisal and instant offer

We built the fix for each one inside our own stores, in front of our own reps, and kept what worked. The scoreboard on the wall, the deal desk, the transport lanes, Colebot answering Messenger at 11 PM and the assistant that reads the morning numbers were all in use on our floor before they had a name.

Now we host it for other dealers, on the same terms we demanded for ourselves: one system, one bill, buy what you need, and every byte of your data yours to keep.
